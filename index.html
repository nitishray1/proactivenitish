The Process

Structured. Private.
Built around you.

Every session follows a clear process — education, honest conversation, a proper wellness check, and a plan that actually fits your life. Then we follow up every four weeks to keep it real.

  1. 1

    Education first

    Nitish walks you through the key wellness areas that matter — sleep, blood sugar, heart health, stress, nutrition, and lifestyle — using a structured booklet so nothing gets missed and everything is explained clearly.

  2. 2

    Your concerns, your priorities

    Once you understand the landscape, you share what matters most to you — energy, weight, sleep, digestion, stress, or anything else. No agenda, no assumptions. This is your session.

  3. 3

    A proper wellness check

    Nitish conducts a simple body composition check and nutrition assessment to give you a clear starting point — real numbers, not guesses — so progress can be measured, not just felt.

  4. 4

    A plan that fits your actual week

    From everything discussed, Nitish builds one focused, realistic plan — not a list of twenty things, just the most important next step. Then follows up every four weeks to adjust, encourage, and keep you moving.

Questions

Things people usually ask

If something isn't answered here, just reach out. Happy to have a quick chat before you commit to anything at all.

Who is this coaching for? +
Busy working people — those with full-time jobs, long commutes, and limited energy who want to feel better without a complete life overhaul. If you've tried big programs and they haven't stuck, this is built for people exactly like you.
What does a session actually cover? +
Whatever is most relevant to you right now — sleep, nutrition basics, movement, energy, stress, or hydration. The goal is always one clear, small action you can actually take this week. No information overload, no homework you'll resent.
Is this medical advice? +
No. Nitish offers general wellness coaching and education. He is not a doctor and does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe anything. For medical concerns, always speak to a qualified GP or healthcare professional first.
How much time does this actually take? +
Sessions are conversational and focused — typically 30 to 60 minutes. The habits built together are designed to take minutes, not hours. If something doesn't fit your real week, it gets made smaller until it does.
Can I do this online or does it have to be in person? +
Both are available. Nitish is based in Sydney so in-person sessions are possible locally. Online coaching is also available for people elsewhere in Australia or internationally.
What if I don't follow through between sessions? +
That's part of the conversation — not something to hide. Real coaching includes honest check-ins about what didn't happen and why. The plan adjusts, no shame involved. If the habit didn't work, that's useful information — not a failure.
