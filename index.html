About Nitish

Built from real life, not a perfect routine

I know what it's like to work full time, commute long hours, and still try to take care of yourself. I'm not a wellness influencer with a perfect morning routine. I'm someone who's figured out what actually works when life is messy and energy is low.

That's what I help people with — finding the smallest, most realistic version of better that you can actually stick to. Not the version that sounds impressive. The version that fits your actual week.

My approach is built on honest conversation, simple habit focus, and follow-up support — not overwhelming programs or generic advice. Everything stays practical, private, and pressure-free.

Questions

Things people usually ask

If something isn't answered here, just reach out — happy to have a quick chat before you commit to anything.

Who is this coaching for? +
Busy working people — those with full-time jobs, long commutes, and limited energy who want to feel better without a complete life overhaul. If you've tried big programs and they haven't stuck, this is built for people like you.
What does a session actually cover? +
We focus on what's most relevant to you — sleep, nutrition basics, movement, energy, stress, or hydration. The goal is always one clear, small action you can actually take this week. No information overload.
Is this medical advice? +
No. This is general wellness coaching and education. Nitish is not a doctor and does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. For medical concerns, always speak to a qualified GP or healthcare professional.
How much time does this take? +
The sessions themselves are conversational and focused — typically 30 to 60 minutes. The habits we build together are designed to take minutes, not hours. If it doesn't fit your real week, we make it smaller.
Can I do this online or does it have to be in person? +
Both are available. Nitish is based in Sydney, so in-person sessions are possible locally. Online coaching is also available for people elsewhere in Australia or internationally.
What if I don't follow through between sessions? +
That's part of the conversation — not something to hide. Real coaching includes honest check-ins about what didn't happen and why. We adjust, we don't shame. If the habit didn't work, that's useful information.
